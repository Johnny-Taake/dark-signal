# Weapon handling and grenade audio

These edited clips are distributed under **CC0 1.0**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- `pump-back.wav`, `pump-forward.wav`, `shell-in.wav`: **zer0_sol**, [Shotgun Reload Sound effects](https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects). Extracted from `Rack.mp3` and `Shell in Chamber.mp3` in `shotgunsounds.zip`.
- `pistol-out.wav`, `pistol-in.wav`, `pistol-bolt.wav`: **zer0_sol**, [Handgun Reload Sound Effect](https://opengameart.org/content/handgun-reload-sound-effect), original `reload.wav`.
- `ar-out.wav`, `ar-in.wav`, `ar-bolt.wav`: **SpringySpringo**, [Gun reload sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds), original `assaultriflereload1_0.wav`. The author describes recordings made with airsoft guns.

Adaptations: separate action segments, silence trimming, mono 22.05 kHz PCM conversion, 90 Hz high-pass / 5.2 kHz low-pass filtering and short edge fades. The AR clips play at 0.92 speed with a darker filter for a heavier mechanism. Existing firing sounds are independent and unchanged by this audio replacement.

`grenade-explosion.wav`: **Joth**, [Chunky Explosion](https://opengameart.org/content/chunky-explosion), original `Chunky Explosion.mp3`, **CC0 1.0**. The author labels this a pseudorealistic sound effect, not a documented live grenade recording. Adaptations: shortened to 1.6 seconds, mono 24 kHz PCM, 45 Hz high-pass / 6 kHz low-pass, 3 dB reduction around 2.6 kHz, 2 ms attack fade and 400 ms tail fade. Playback adds distance attenuation, stereo direction and cover muffling.
