“LONG CALL” PROTOCOL

Hold the relay against attacking monsters. Each transmission draws more. Between pulses, the emergency unit supplies ammunition. In a squad, fallen operators respawn after 10 seconds while a teammate survives. If everyone falls, the transmission ends; the host restarts or returns the whole squad to the menu.
